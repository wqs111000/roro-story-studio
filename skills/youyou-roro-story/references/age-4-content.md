# Four-year-old content guidance

## Default shape

- Spoken length: about 3-6 minutes.
- Pages: exactly 6, with one main action or discovery per page. The cover is separate and is numbered page 0 in production records.
- Sentences: mostly short and concrete; explain uncommon words through action.
- Plot: one small problem, a few attempts, a safe resolution, and a calm ending.
- Interaction: zero to two simple choices, sounds, gestures, or prediction questions.
- Learning: one main theme; optional natural exposure to a few words or facts.

## Emotional and safety boundaries

- Keep danger imaginary, brief, and clearly resolved with a trusted path to safety.
- Avoid abandonment threats, graphic injury, death detail, humiliation, body shame, and prolonged suspense.
- Do not diagnose feelings or label a child as naughty, cowardly, selfish, or bad.
- Allow anger, fear, sadness, and jealousy; model naming the feeling and choosing a safe next action.
- Do not require secrecy from parents or trusted caregivers.
- Do not turn animal size, species, disability, appearance, or appetite into moral worth.
- Dinosaurs, tigers, lions, snakes, and wolves need not be villains. Give behavior a concrete reason.
- End bedtime stories with lower energy, familiar sensory detail, and reassurance.

## Participation patterns

Useful participation includes repeating a three-to-six-word phrase, making an animal sound, pointing to an object, choosing between two safe paths, or taking one slow breath with Roro. Participation should remain optional and should not block the story.
